# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) v2.1.

In brief: we are building infrastructure for some of the most underserved health systems in the world. Be kind, be precise, assume good faith, and remember that the people relying on this software may never meet us — our conduct in this community is part of the product.

Reports: conduct@openhealthos.org. All reports are reviewed confidentially.
